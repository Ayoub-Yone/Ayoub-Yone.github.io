Why Yone?

Yone is a fighter/assassin with high mobility. He deals both physical and magic damage, has excellent scaling potential, and his kit lets him play side lanes before making a major impact in teamfights.

Pressure

Side lanes are his playground: he pushes waves, forces enemy responses and opens up space for the team.

Mobility

Dashes, repositioning and ranged engages: he picks his fights, breaks away and repositions where his impact is strongest.

Scaling

His damage ramps up with items: real potential in mid and late game, provided he gets there in good conditions.

Teamfight

A well-timed flank, a clean engage and burst damage: when the timing is right, he can change the outcome of a fight.

Note for the jungler

“No need to force TOP ganks to get me playing. I can handle my lane and play for my scaling. However, counter-ganks and cover when the enemy jungler plays around TOP are particularly useful.”

No forced gank

My lane can live without a visit: I farm, secure my waves and play for my scaling. Your time is better spent elsewhere.

Counter-gank = useful

When the enemy jungler shows up around TOP, a ping, cover or a counter-gank really changes the course of the game.

Timing first

Good timing beats a forced gank: objectives, resets and teamfight windows come before a visit with no advantage.
